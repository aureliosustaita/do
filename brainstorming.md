Mobile App Dev - App Brainstorming Example

New App Ideas - List

To-Do AppA productivity app for students that not only tracks tasks but also features a "Motivation Bank" showing past completed work to help users stay inspired. Users can add notes, due dates, and sort by course or category.

TravelBuddyAn app that connects solo travelers in the same city and suggests local attractions or restaurants to visit together. Uses geolocation and in-app messaging for coordination.

Study Group FinderAn education-focused app for students to find, join, or host virtual or in-person study groups based on subject, course, or shared goals. Includes RSVP and messaging features.

MindSpaA health & fitness app that offers quick daily mental health check-ins, audio-guided meditations, mood journaling, and breathing exercises. Uses notifications to encourage daily use.

FitSnapA fitness tracker that integrates with your phone camera to capture progress photos and automatically create side-by-side comparisons and progress timelines.

Social Bucket ListA social app that allows users to create bucket lists and invite friends to complete items together. Includes a photo journal, map pins of visited places, and group chat.

EventPalA lifestyle app that finds local events (e.g., farmers markets, concerts, book clubs) based on user interests and availability. Syncs with calendar and allows bookmarking/favoriting.

New App Ideas - Evaluate and Categorize

1. To-Do App with Motivation Bank
Description: A task manager that logs completed tasks into a Motivation Bank. This encourages users to reflect on past progress when they feel unmotivated.
Category: Productivity
Mobile: Uses push notifications and touch interactions for adding/deleting tasks. Could integrate with reminders and haptics.
Story: Helps users stay organized and feel good about themselves by showing their own past successes. Especially great for students or anyone who struggles with motivation.
Market: Students, young professionals, anyone with ADHD or anxiety who benefits from encouragement.
Habit: Users return daily to check tasks, and visit Motivation Bank during moments of stress or burnout.
Scope: MVP can include basic CRUD for tasks and a separate screen to view completed tasks. V2 can include streaks, quotes, and voice notes.
2. Quiet Space Finder
Description: Helps users find, review, and bookmark quiet places for working, reading, or studying, based on crowd-sourced noise ratings and real-time occupancy updates.
Category: Lifestyle / Productivity
Mobile: Utilizes geolocation, maps, camera (for photos), and reviews.
Story: In a noisy world, users want peace — this gives them personalized quiet zones. Useful for remote workers, students, writers, and introverts.
Market: Urban dwellers, college students, freelancers, and neurodivergent individuals.
Habit: Users may check the app before heading out or planning study sessions.
Scope: MVP includes map view and user-submitted reviews. V2 can add filters, real-time status, and social features.
Final App Decision

Chosen App: To-Do App with Motivation Bank
Why?
This app is highly relevant to students, unique in its emphasis on emotional reinforcement, and achievable within the timeframe. Its mobile-first features and clear expansion roadmap make it a great capstone project for this course.
